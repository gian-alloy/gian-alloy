# 👋🏻 Hi there — I’m Gian Paolo (GP)

I lead the design and development of **agentic AI applications at [Alloy Therapeutics](https://alloytx.com/)** — tools that help scientists explore experimental, proprietary, and research data and turn it into actionable scientific insights.

I love owning things end to end: from a first idea with a scientist, to a hardened, monitored, production-grade AI product people actually use every day.

## 🚀 What I’m working on
- **Agentic AI**: designing and building AI agents on modern LLM platforms and agentic architectures
- **Model customization**: evaluating fine-tuning and optimization strategies with Python and PyTorch
- **Trustworthy AI**: evaluation frameworks, monitoring, security controls, and governance for regulated scientific environments
- **Cloud & data**: integrating AI services with AWS infrastructure and enterprise data platforms
- **Engineering discipline**: CI/CD, automated testing, and deployments that scale

## 🧬 How I work
- Side by side with scientists, bioinformaticians, product teams, and engineers to find the high-impact AI opportunities
- Mentoring engineers and helping shape our long-term AI platform strategy
- The goal: next-generation AI assistants that speed up scientific discovery and make everyone’s day a little easier
